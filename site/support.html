Adding the widgets

Open Pax once, so it can fill the widgets. Then press and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and choose Pax. For the Lock Screen, press and hold it, tap Customize → Lock Screen, then tap the widget area and choose Pax.

The Prayer widget can show a prayer for the hour of the day or today's Rosary: press and hold it and tap Edit Widget.

A widget looks out of date

Widgets update whenever you open Pax or finish something in it, and turn over on their own at midnight. If one seems stuck, open Pax for a moment.

Reminders don't arrive

Check Me → Reminders in Pax, and make sure notifications for Pax are allowed in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications. Focus modes can hold reminders back too.

Which calendar does Pax follow?

Pax follows the liturgical calendar of the United States, including its holy days and saints. The readings are shown in the Douay-Rheims Bible; Pax links to the full official readings online.

Your data

Everything you do in Pax stays on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it. See the privacy policy.
