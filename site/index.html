Pray, read and study with the Church, every day.

Pax is a daily Catholic companion for iPhone. It follows the liturgical year: the feast and saint of the day, today's Mass readings, the Rosary and other devotions, reading plans and the Douay-Rheims Bible.

Read

Today's Mass readings in the Douay-Rheims Bible, the saint of the day, and the whole Bible with Bishop Challoner's notes, the Church Fathers and a guide to the Catechism.

Pray

The Rosary one bead at a time, chaplets, the Stations of the Cross, litanies, novenas, a nightly Examen and a prayer book of traditional prayers.

Study

Reading plans, from a Gospel in a few weeks to the Bible in a year, and guided reading of the Fathers, starting with Saint Ignatius of Antioch.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. What you do in Pax stays on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
